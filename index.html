    🎼 The Musical Staff & Landmark Guideposts

    Music is written on 5 lines and 4 spaces. Use the 3 Conservatory Landmarks to orient instantly:

    • Treble G (G4 — Line 2): Sits inside the curl of the 𝄞 G-Clef.
    • Middle C (C4 — Center Ledger): Bridges Treble and Bass staves.
    • Bass F (F3 — Line 4): Sits between the two dots of the 𝄢 F-Clef.
    • Treble Spaces (F-A-C-E): Spells "FACE" from bottom to top.

    ⏱️ Rhythm, Meter & Note Values

    Every note duration represents beats of time (at 96 BPM):

    • Whole Note (𝅝): 4 beats — ring and hold.
    • Dotted Half (𝅗𝅥.): 3 beats — waltz pulse.
    • Half Note (𝅗𝅥): 2 beats — half measure.
    • Quarter Note (♩): 1 beat — steady heartbeat.
    • Eighth Notes (♫): ½ beat each — two per beat.

    🖐️ Ergonomics, Hand Posture & Fingerings

    Healthy piano technique prevents strain and unlocks speed:

    • Arched Fingers: Imagine holding a bubble or tennis ball gently over the keys.
    • Thumb Tuck (1 under 3): How your 5 fingers smoothly walk across an 8-note scale.
    • Arm Weight: Let gravity drop from relaxed shoulders rather than stiffly poking fingers.
    • Fingers 1 to 5: 1 = Thumb • 2 = Index • 3 = Middle • 4 = Ring • 5 = Pinky.

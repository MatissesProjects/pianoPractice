🎼 The Musical Staff & Clefs

Music is written on 5 lines and 4 spaces.

  • Treble Clef (𝄞): Lines: E - G - B - D - F ("Every Good Boy Does Fine"). Spaces: F - A - C - E.
  • Bass Clef (𝄢): Lines: G - B - D - F - A ("Good Boys Do Fine Always"). Spaces: A - C - E - G ("All Cows Eat Grass").
  • Middle C (C4): Anchors between both clefs on its own ledger line.

⏱️ Rhythm & Note Values

Every note duration represents beats of time:

  • Whole Note (𝅝): 4 beats — ring and hold.
  • Half Note (𝅗𝅥): 2 beats — half duration.
  • Quarter Note (♩): 1 beat — steady heartbeat.
  • Eighth Note (♪): ½ beat — two per quarter note.
  • Dotted Note (♩.): Adds 50% more duration (1.5 beats).

🖐️ Ergonomics & Hand Posture

Healthy piano technique prevents strain and enhances speed:

  • Arched Fingers: Imagine holding a gentle tennis ball.
  • Relaxed Wrists: Keep wrists flexible and level with keys.
  • Arm Weight: Drop arm weight from shoulders rather than pushing from fingers.
  • Fingers 1 to 5: 1 = Thumb, 2 = Index, 3 = Middle, 4 = Ring, 5 = Pinky.

🎹 Harmonic Dyads & Intervals

Intervals measure the musical distance between notes:

  • Half Step (Semitone): Closest key adjacent (white to black, or E-F, B-C).
  • Whole Step (Tone): Two half steps (C to D, F to G).
  • Major 3rd: 4 half steps (C to E) — bright and sweet.
  • Minor 3rd: 3 half steps (A to C) — moody and somber.
  • Perfect 5th: 7 half steps (C to G) — powerful and resonant.
